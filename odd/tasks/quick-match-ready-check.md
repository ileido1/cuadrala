# Quick match ready check (cola tipo League of Legends)

Objective: quick match forms full groups of 4 and runs a simultaneous accept/decline ready check, like the League of Legends queue. Branch `feat/quick-match-ready-check` (from `128f4cc`). Delivery: `ask-on-risk`. Runners: API `npm test` (Vitest; integration needs `TEST_DATABASE_URL`), mobile `flutter test`.

## Specs

- **S1 — Lobby that fills individually (user, L10–L12, supersedes the "group only when full" rule).** User's words: "claro que se juega de 4 pero se pueden ir uniendo indiviudalmente". When a player enters the queue and another compatible player (same sport, category, target date, overlapping slots) is also searching, they are put together in the same lobby immediately, with no acceptance needed (both pressed search). Further compatible searchers join the existing lobby (oldest lobby first) until it has 4. A lobby with members open is filled before a new lobby is formed. API contract: a lobby member has search status `SEARCHING` and a proposal `{ type: NEW_GROUP, status: LOBBY, playerIds: current members (2–3), groupKey }`; `acceptedCount` 0. A player alone has no proposal (`noMatchYet: true`).
- **S12 — Availability invitations (opt-in).** Users whose saved availability matches an open lobby receive an invitation ("Hay un grupo armándose a tu horario, ¿te sumás?"). They join the lobby only if they accept. Contract: invitee search status `PROPOSAL`, proposal `{ type: NEW_GROUP, status: PENDING, playerIds: lobby members (fewer than 4, not including the invitee until accepted is fine as long as documented), expiresAt: now + HOLD_MS }`, `invitedByAvailability: true`. Accept (`/proposal/confirm`) → joins the lobby (or triggers S2 if it makes 4). Decline → `CANCELLED`; no answer in time → `EXPIRED`; neither is invited again the same date. Clients tell an invitation from a ready check by the search-level `invitedByAvailability` flag (a ready-check member whose group lost someone is also `PENDING` with fewer than 4 `playerIds`).
- **S13 — Lobby notifications.** When a player joins a lobby, the members already in it are notified ("Se sumó un jugador (N de 4)"), in-app + push. A new notification type may be added (additive enum migration).
- **S14 — Leaving a lobby.** A member who leaves (`DELETE`) becomes `CANCELLED`; the lobby continues with the rest. If only one member remains, that member goes back to plain searching (no proposal). Remaining members are notified only if cheap; not required.
- **S2 — Ready check to all 4 at once.** When a lobby reaches 4 members (a searcher joins or an invitee accepts), all 4 members get proposal `PENDING` with the same `expiresAt` (existing `HOLD_MS`, 2 min). Nobody counts as accepted until they accept. All members except the searcher who triggered the match get a `QUICK_MATCH_PROPOSAL` notification (in-app + push), including active searchers pulled into the group (today they are not notified) and availability invitees.
- **S3 — Accept.** `POST /quick-match/proposal/confirm` marks only that member `CONFIRMED`. When all 4 are `CONFIRMED`, the match is created.
- **S4 — Venue auto-selection (user chose option "A").** When the 4th member accepts, the API books the first `venueOptions` entry whose court/time is still free (no active reservation or match), with no venue selection required from the client. If none is free (or there are no options), the match is created without a court. The confirm body's optional venue selection stays accepted for backward compatibility; `CANCHA_REQUERIDA` is no longer thrown.
- **S5 — Decline / timeout: replace only that player (user, L7).** User's words: "si se le vence el tiempo debe buscar a otra persona para que acepte y a esa persona solo sacarla de la cola". A member who does not accept before `expiresAt` leaves the queue (`EXPIRED`); a member who declines (`/proposal/dismiss`) or leaves (`DELETE`) leaves the queue (`CANCELLED`) — assumption: decline follows the same replace-only rule as timeout. The group does NOT dissolve: members who accepted stay `CONFIRMED` in the group; members still pending within their time stay `PENDING`. The freed slot(s) are refilled with the next compatible players (oldest `queuedAt` first among `SEARCHING` searchers, then availability invitees), who get `PENDING` with a fresh `expiresAt` and a `QUICK_MATCH_PROPOSAL` notification. If no replacement exists yet, the group waits with open slots and is refilled when a compatible player enters the queue (an existing group with open slots takes precedence over forming a new one). When all 4 current members are `CONFIRMED`, the match is created (S4). Expiry is resolved for the whole group whenever any member reads their search. Legacy groups from the old incremental logic (fewer than 4, possibly all `CONFIRMED`, expired) are refilled the same way instead of staying stuck (verifier finding A1).
- **S6 — Queue position (priority).** Searchers are matched oldest-in-queue first (`queuedAt`). Members who stay in a group through a refill keep their `queuedAt`; under the replace-only S5 there is no re-queue after a failed ready check, and "Volver a buscar" starts a fresh position. Requires a `queuedAt` timestamp on the search set when the player enters the queue and preserved on re-queue (`updatedAt` changes on every write, so it cannot be used).
- **S7 — Honest status in API.** The search DTO exposes how many group members have accepted (`acceptedCount`) so clients never claim acceptance that did not happen.
- **S8 — OPEN_MATCH unchanged.** "Sumarme a partidas ya armadas" (OPEN_MATCH proposals) keeps its current single-player accept flow.
- **S9 — Leaving the queue (mobile).** "Salir de la cola" lands on home without opening "¿Cuándo quieres jugar?". The quick-match screen never opens the configuration sheet by itself; its idle state shows a button to start a search. User report: "salir de la cola me lleva aqui creo que esta mal hecho ese flujo".
- **S10 — Ready-check UI (mobile).** While the group has open slots (fewer than 4 members in `proposal.playerIds`), the waiting state says it is looking for a replacement player instead of a countdown for others. A player who timed out or declined is out of the queue; their screen offers "Volver a buscar" and "Volver al inicio" (user L7). The NEW_GROUP proposal screen shows "¡Partida encontrada!", a countdown to `expiresAt`, "X de 4 aceptaron" from `acceptedCount`, and Accept/Decline. No venue/time picker. It never shows text claiming players accepted when they did not (today: "Tres jugadores compatibles quieren jugar…", "Elegí sede y horario"). User report: "en el tlf si me lleva como que laguien aceptoo y me muestra a elegir sede y horario cuando nadie ha aceptado". After accepting, it shows waiting for the others; when the match exists, it shows the confirmed match.
- **S11 — Updates without push (mobile).** Proposal and waiting states poll the search so acceptance by others, dissolution, and match creation appear without push. The home banner polls while a search is active and shows "¡Partida encontrada!" when a ready check is pending. User report: "en la sesion que tengo en la pc no me llega la notificacion para aceptar o no la partida".

- **S15 — Web push unsupported browsers (mobile).** On browsers where Firebase Messaging is unsupported (Sentry CUADRALA-MOBILE-5 `messaging/unsupported-browser`, e.g. iOS Safari without installing the app, in-app browsers), push is reported as unavailable instead of throwing an unhandled error.

Out of scope (pending user input): overlapping widgets/missing gaps — awaiting a screenshot of the affected screen.
